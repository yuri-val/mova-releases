How it works

Three steps that take a fraction of a second after every word.

  1. 1

    You type

    Mova sees the keys you press and keeps only the current word in memory.

  2. 2

    The word ends

    Mova reads the same keys in the English and the Ukrainian layout and checks both readings.

  3. 3

    Wrong layout

    Mova erases the word, switches the layout and types the right reading. You carry on in the right language.

⌃+⌥

The hotkey for manual fixes and undo. Pick another in settings: ⌥⇧, ⌃⇧, ⌘⌥ or ⌃⌘.

Settings without the clutter

A menu-bar icon, three settings tabs, and nothing you have to configure before you start. The app itself speaks Ukrainian.

What you type stays on your Mac

Mova sees your keyboard, so privacy is not a detail.

  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored: only the current and the last word are kept in memory.
  • In password fields (secure input) Mova sees no keys at all.
  • The network is used only for updates and, once a day, for the license: the key and a hash of the Mac’s identifier that cannot be turned back into its serial number. No analytics, no accounts.
  • Builds are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple; updates are EdDSA-signed.

Set up in a minute

Mova needs two macOS permissions; without them it sees nothing and changes nothing.

  1. Download Mova.dmg, open it and drag Mova into Applications.
  2. Launch Mova: an «AЯ» icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. In System Settings → Privacy & Security turn Mova on under Input Monitoring (to see keys) and Accessibility (to retype the word).
  4. Type. No restart needed: Mova picks the permissions up within seconds.

Questions

Mova doesn't fix anything. What should I check?

Open the Mova menu: the status line should say it is listening, with two permission ticks. Also check that auto-switching is on and the app is not in the exclusion list.

Mova fixed a word I meant to keep.

Press ⌃⌥ right away: the word comes back and won't be fixed automatically again.

Mova didn't fix a word.

Press ⌃⌥ to fix it by hand. If the word comes up often, add it to the always-switch list.

Which layouts do I need?

English and Ukrainian layouts enabled in macOS, for example U.S. and Ukrainian-PC. Mova leaves other layouts alone.

Does it work in terminals and code editors?

Not automatically: ordinary text there often looks like a typo. The hotkey works everywhere, and the exclusion list is yours to edit.

How do I uninstall Mova?

Quit it from the menu and move Mova from Applications to the Trash. Settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/Mova/.

Does Mova need the internet?

How do I move my license to a new Mac?

Activate the key on the new Mac. If there are no free seats, click Settings → License → Deactivate on the old one, or just wait: a Mac silent for 30 days frees its seat.
