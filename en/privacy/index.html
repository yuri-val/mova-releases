Privacy policy

Version of 2 November 2026. Data controller: Yurii Valihurskyi, sole proprietor (FOP) (taxpayer number 3455107130), 65069, Ukraine, Odesa region, Odesa, Kapitana Kuznietsova St, bldg. 110, apt. 43, support@yuri-val.dev.

The Mova app

  • Mova asks macOS for two permissions. Input Monitoring — to see the keys you press and recognise a word typed in the wrong layout. Accessibility — to erase that word and type it in the other layout. Mova uses these permissions for nothing else.
  • What you type is never sent or stored: only the current and the previous word are kept in memory. Mova sees no keys in password fields.
  • Once a day Mova checks GitHub for updates and confirms the license with our server. For the license it sends the key, a pseudonymised Mac identifier (a hash of the hardware identifier that cannot be turned back into the identifier or the serial number) and the Mova version. The hash is linked to your key, so we treat it as personal data.
  • No analytics, no ads, no accounts. Mova sends no crash reports of its own; if Apple analytics sharing is on in macOS, Apple handles those reports.

The site

  • The site uses no analytics and no cookies. Your theme choice is kept only in your browser.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any host, GitHub sees the visitor’s IP address.

What we process, why and on what basis

  • Purchase: email, chosen edition, amount, payment status and the monobank invoice number — to deliver and support the license (performance of the contract) and keep the sole proprietor’s accounts (legal obligation). Card data is processed by the bank; we never see it.
  • License: key, email, pseudonymised Mac identifiers, activation and last check dates — to count Macs and recover keys (performance of the contract).
  • Contact form: name, email and message — to reply (your request); IP address — against spam (our legitimate interest), kept for one day.

Who else sees the data

  • JSC Universal Bank (monobank), Ukraine — payments.
  • Cloudflare, Inc., USA — license server and database (hosted in the EU).
  • Resend, USA — sending email (EU region).
  • GitHub, Inc., USA — the site and update files (hosted in the USA).

These providers process data on our behalf. Transfers outside Ukraine and the EU are needed to perform the contract and rely on standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework under the providers’ data processing terms. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Retention

Purchase and license data are kept while the license is valid and as long as tax accounting requires. Contact messages — until the question is resolved. IP addresses for spam protection — one day.

Age

Licenses are to be bought by people aged 18 or over, or with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

Your rights

Under the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” (and the GDPR for EU residents) you can ask what data we hold, correct it, ask us to delete it or object to processing, and complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. Write to support@yuri-val.dev. Deleting license data means revoking the license.

Changes

We update this page when data processing changes and show the date of the new version.
